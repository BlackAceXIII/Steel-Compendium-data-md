# Biography
- Edgar came from the lands of the Norse gods, with his mentor on a viking ship.
- Edgar was taught some dark magic by this mentor who, unbeknownst to Edgar, was running from Hela as he had escaped the underworld.
- Edgar's mentor eventually betrayed the crew and killed everyone to steal their vitality and might.
- Edgar's soul went to the realms of Hades, and he was selected by the lords of the underworld to go back into the world of the living to put an end to his mentor's rampage.
- Edgar now wields the magic of Hades because his strength in life was stolen by his mentor.
